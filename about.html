About

Why this site exists

Most meal-prep content is written for people who already have a system. Sunday Shelf is written for people who don't — who want dinner handled without turning Sunday into a second job.

The approach here is narrow on purpose: one batch-cooking method, a short pantry list, and a handful of techniques that actually save time instead of just looking impressive. Every guide states its ingredients up front, in the amounts you actually need, and every recipe assumes you're cooking once and eating from it for several days.

Who writes this

Sunday Shelf is an independent, ad-supported publication. There's no sponsorship arrangement with any brand mentioned here, and no ingredient is recommended because someone paid for it to be.
